About the forum

Omics for Health (omics4health) is an online academic forum founded by a group of early-career faculty working in gut microbiome research and mass spectrometry-based omics, including proteomics and metabolomics.

Our organizers are primarily Chinese researchers. We meet approximately once a month to share research, discuss methods and exchange ideas with colleagues across these fields.

The forum provides a regular space for scientific discussion and for building connections between researchers interested in omics and health.

Participate

Researchers and students with an interest in these areas are welcome to join our talks. Please contact us if you would like to attend, suggest a speaker or help organize a session.
